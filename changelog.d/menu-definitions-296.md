- Every object kind has one menu, and every way of opening it shows the same rows in the same order: the ⋮ button, a right-click on the page header or the sidebar row, and the workspace chip on the rail (Feature #296, Issues #761, #764).
- Menus run in four sections, Settings, Organize, Share and Export, with Delete last. Export is the one labelled section: each row names a format (Markdown, Mermaid, HTML, PDF, CSV, JSON) with its extension beside it.
- Rows that belong elsewhere left the menus. **Stats** is a toolbar button beside Fields, and **Sort** shows on the desktop toolbar too, so a column's ⋮ no longer sorts. A record's Activity and Show or hide fields buttons, and its copy link, show in the dock and on a phone. Rename a table by editing its title or double-clicking its sidebar row; change a workspace logo by clicking it on the workspace page. Link preview before sign-in is a switch in the workspace menu. A deck's download, model, and new version controls are buttons on its panel. Column settings read **Field settings…**.
