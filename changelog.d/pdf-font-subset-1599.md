- **A PDF embeds only the DejaVu glyphs it uses** (Issue #1599): one arrow or emoji used to embed and deflate the whole 757 KB DejaVu Sans on every render, so a 1.6 KB document came out near 390 KB in about 14 ms. The writer now builds a TrueType subset that keeps the used glyphs and their composite parts under their own glyph ids, with a tagged font name, so the same document is about 16 KB in under a millisecond. A table cell too wide for its column now ends in an ellipsis instead of stopping mid-word.
