- The workspace home opens as a launcher on a phone, the same one a space opens as: a search box over every table, one tile per space with its record count, recent views and records from any space, automation runs across the workspace with any failed step, and About this workspace for the description, the system tables, saved pages and the relation map. Desktop and tablet widths keep the current home, and a workspace with no tables keeps its onboarding card (Feature #355).
- `GET /api/workspace/home` answers the spaces, tables, counts, views and runs the launcher draws, with the same private-space and share-link rules as `GET /api/spaces/:id/home` (Feature #355).
