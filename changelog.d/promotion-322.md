- **Production promotes itself from green main** (Feature #322): the new `promote` workflow runs hourly and acts every `PROMOTION_EVERY_HOURS` (default 2) or on dispatch. It picks the newest main commit with a `CI gate` success and a `dev-soak` success that descends from the `production` branch, fast-forwards `production` to it through the promotion deploy key, calls prod's supervisor deploy hook and waits for `/api/health` to show that commit with its ten-minute watch passed. A failed watch or a rollback fails the run, files an Issue row, alerts and opens a revert PR on main for the promoted range; `production` is never rewound. `PROMOTION_PAUSED=true` stops it. Gate: `test/promote.test.mjs`.
