- Every drag-to-reorder surface shares one treatment, with a mouse and on phones: grid column headers, the Fields popover, view tabs, group levels, workflow states, select options (new), entity page fields and blocks, and list view rows. On pickup the item lifts with a slight scale and a deeper shadow; the item itself waits, dimmed, in a dashed placeholder at the slot it will land in while its neighbours slide aside; the drop settles it into the placeholder, and Escape or a drop outside sends it home. On touch a 250 ms long press lifts it, with a haptic tick where the platform offers one. The list auto-scrolls near its edges and stops once the pointer leaves the edge band. With reduced motion the placeholder stays and the scale and slide go. Keyboard reorder is unchanged (Feature #282).
