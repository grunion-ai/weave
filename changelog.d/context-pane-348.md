- **Activity opens in the context pane, the next card in the pane row** (Feature #348): a new `#side` card sits after `#dock` with its own `#side-gutter`, built from the entity pane's parts. It is the same card (16 px radius, panel shadow, 8 px margins), right of the record on the full page and right of the entity pane in the peek, so it covers neither. Its width defaults to 340 px; dragging the gutter pins it (min 300 px, kept as `wv-side-width`) and a double-click resets it. The URL carries `side=activity` beside `?e=`, so a reload or a shared link reopens it, and ⌘⇧E between the full page and the peek keeps it open. Esc closes it before the entity pane, and closing the record closes it. On a phone it is still the full-screen sheet with a history step. This replaces the pane docked inside the record view (Issues #871, #906): the record keeps its full width and the margin-right offsets are gone. `pinDock`, `applyDockWidth` and `wireDockGutter` became `pinPane`, `applyPaneWidth` and `wirePaneGutter`, shared by both panes. Gate: `test/activity-panel-placement-browser.test.mjs` (6 cases), `test/phone-activity-sheet-browser.test.mjs`.
