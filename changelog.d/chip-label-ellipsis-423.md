- **A value chip longer than its column ends in an ellipsis inside its fill** (Issue #423): in a narrow column a select chip ran its text past its own fill and the cell cut it mid-letter ("Chase Ultimate Re" on uno Points Balances). `.k` is inline-flex, so the text was an anonymous flex item that could not shrink, and the cell's text-overflow never reaches text inside an inline-flex child. Select, state, multi-select and key chips now carry their text in a `.k-label` span that truncates with an ellipsis, the rule relation chips already used; a grid chip clips at its fill; a multi-select box is capped at its column while chips beside each other keep their widths. A cut label marks its cell clipped, so the hover expansion still opens, and carries the whole value as its title. Gate: `test/chip-ellipsis-browser.test.mjs`, both themes.
