- Automations: a `webhook-inbound` trigger on a Workflows record. Saving the rule mints `POST /w/<workspace>/api/hooks/<id>`; `weave automation secret` shows its secret once (`--rotate` mints a new one). A POST is admitted with an `X-Weave-Signature` over `<timestamp>.<body>` and a timestamp within five minutes, the scheme weave's signed webhooks send, or with the secret as `X-Weave-Secret`. Bodies over 64 KB answer 413, a redelivered key answers 200 and runs nothing, a change weave just wrote to the sender (`from`) is dropped, and an Off rule answers 202. The body is `trigger.body`, each item of `items` is `trigger.item`, and `record` names the record set-field and add-comment write to. Every delivery is logged on the rule with its time, status, size and outcome, and the last one shows in Health Reason (Feature #227).
