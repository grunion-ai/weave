- **The frozen # pair stays opaque on the row open in the dock** (Issue #409): Kyle scrolled uno > Buyer > Shopping List sideways and read `$1,119` printed across `#1 ↗`. The row was the one open in the dock. The docked row's light is `background: var(--tblr-active-bg)` on every cell, and in the light theme that token is 4% of the accent over nothing; the rule out-ranked the frozen pair's opaque ground (Issue #252), so the checkbox and # cells turned into a window onto the cells scrolling under them, tint laid on tint. It happened in Chromium and WebKit alike, and never in the dark theme, whose active colour is opaque. The docked row's frozen cells now lay the same light over the surface (`linear-gradient(active) surface`), and a field frozen beside # (Feature #233) does the same through the grid's layout sheet. Gate: `test/frozen-ground-browser.test.mjs` (new, 20 cases, in the gate's browser and in WebKit when installed): a Shopping-List-shaped grid scrolled until a currency value sits under the # link, in light and dark at 1024x600 and 390x844, samples every pixel of the frozen pair outside its checkbox and link against the row's ground past the seam, for a row under the pointer, the docked row at rest and under the pointer, and a chosen row; plus a docked row with a frozen Name. The PNG reader it needs is `test/lib/png.mjs`, on `node:zlib` alone.
