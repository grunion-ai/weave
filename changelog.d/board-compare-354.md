- **Compare two to four picked Board cards in the pane row** (Feature #354): pick cards with the box that shows on hover or focus, with Shift-click, or on a phone with a long-press, then **Compare N** in the footer. The pane beside the board draws one column per card with its cover on top and the view's shown fields aligned row by row; a row whose values differ is tinted, and every value edits in place. A fifth pick is refused with a footer hint, Esc closes the pane, and on a phone the pane is the full-screen sheet that scrolls sideways one card at a time. Shift-click on a card picks it instead of opening a new tab. Gates: `test/board-compare.test.mjs`, `test/board-compare-browser.test.mjs`.
