- **The field dialog's Clock options read as the clock alone** (Issue #1477): on a date field that stores a time of day, a format with spaces in it (long, month, ordinal, quarter) leaked part of the date into the Clock control, so the options read `8, 2026 14:30` and `8, 2026 2:30 PM`. Each option is now labelled from the clock reading alone, `14:30` and `2:30 PM`, whatever the date format. Gate: `test/date-clock-labels-browser.test.mjs`.
