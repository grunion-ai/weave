- **The built-in passkey door is removed** (Feature #243): people sign in through the OpenID Connect provider (Feature #212) and agents keep `wv_` tokens. Gone: `src/webauthn.js`, the routes `POST /api/auth/register/options`, `/api/auth/register/verify`, `/api/auth/login/options`, `/api/auth/login/verify` and `DELETE /api/auth/credentials/<id>` (all now answer 404), the passkey and "Register this device" parts of `/auth`, the engine's invite and credential verbs, the CLI verbs `weave account invite` and `weave account remove-credential`, the `weave_accounts` actions `invite` and `remove-credential`, and the Handbook guide "Door B: passkeys". Kept: the `wv_session` cookie, `weave account sessions` and `weave account revoke-session`, sign-out, the rate limits, `WEAVE_ORIGIN` (now the origin the provider redirects back to) and `WEAVE_TRUST_PROXY`, share links and the applet passcode. A passkey already stored on an account row stays in the data and is ignored; `weave account list` and `/api/auth/me` no longer show it. Before upgrading, link each person's account to a provider identity (`weave account link <name> --email <address>`), or they cannot sign in in a browser. Gates: `test/auth-passkey-removed.test.mjs`, `test/auth-session.test.mjs`.
- **`/auth` goes straight to the provider** (Feature #243): with `WEAVE_OIDC_ISSUER` and `WEAVE_OIDC_CLIENT_ID` set, a signed-out visit answers `302` to `/api/auth/oidc/start`, keeping a same-site `?next`, with no page in between. Signed in, `/auth` still shows sessions and sign-out; signing out lands on `/auth?signed-out=1`, which shows the provider link rather than bouncing back through the provider's own session. Gate: `test/auth-passkey-removed.test.mjs`.
