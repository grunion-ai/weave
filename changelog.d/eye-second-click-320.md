- **A second click on the eye closes its popover** (Issue #320): Kyle, 2026-09-18, "visibility toggle dialog doesn't close on icon click". `showPopover()` hangs its outside-click listener in the capture phase and counted the trigger itself as outside, so a second click on the eye closed the popover and that same click's own handler opened a fresh one: the dialog never closed. The field menu's ⋮, a Σ row cell and a colour swatch wore the same loop. A click on the trigger now closes its popover and stops there, so every trigger toggles. The eye passes an `owns` option, because a flip redraws the surface and the eye that opened the popover is a detached node by the next click: any eye in the same region (`#main` or `#dock`) counts as its own, while the other surface's eye still opens its own popover in one click. A popover already closed by Escape or a pick only unhooks its listener and lets the click through, so the eye opens on the first click after an Escape. Gates: `test/popover-toggle-browser.test.mjs` (second click closes and stays closed, third opens; Escape and an outside click still close and the eye reopens after either; the eye redrawn by a flip still closes; each in light and dark; the docked entity's eye switches in one click and closes its own; a column's ⋮ toggles and another column's ⋮ switches in one click).
