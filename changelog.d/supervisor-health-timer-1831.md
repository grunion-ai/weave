- **The supervisor's health wait stops its timer once the worker listens** (Issue #1831): `waitHealthy()` raced the worker's `listening` promise against a 60-second sleep it never cancelled, so a process that started a worker and then closed the supervisor stayed alive until the sleep ran out. The timer is now cleared as soon as the race settles, and a worker that never listens is still refused at the timeout. `test/supervisor.test.mjs` runs at the default health timeout again and ends 102 seconds after it starts, down from 165.
