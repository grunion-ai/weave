- **A toggle column is never narrower than its switch and its word** (Issue #586): a column's floor was its header label alone, so a toggle named "On" dragged to 45px while its cell paints a 28px track, a 7px gap and the word of its state inside 4px of padding each side; the word ellipsized and then the track clipped. `settle()` in `public/app.js` now gives every toggle column a value floor, as a date column has had since Issue #159: the track, the gap and the wider of the field's two words, measured in the cell's own face, plus the cell's measured padding (`toggleWidth` and `TOGGLE_METRICS` in `public/column-resize.js`). The floor binds a drag, the Alt+arrow nudge, a double-click fit and the frozen-zone cap, and raises a default or stored width under it for that column only, so a toggle worded "Synchronising every night" opens at 203px where the type default is 124. Gates: `test/column-resize.test.mjs`, `test/toggle-width-browser.test.mjs` (default On / Off, Running / Paused and a word past the default, in an on row and an off row, with every other column's width held).
