- **No select, multi-select, state or rating column is cut by a resize** (Issue #614): these columns floored at their header label only, so a State column dragged under a "Setup incomplete" chip cut the chip mid-word, and a rating dragged under its icons cut the last ones. Kyle, 2026-10-03: "make sure no part of the toggle or box can be cut off by field resize." A select or state now floors at its widest option's chip, a multi-select at its widest chip plus the +N count beside it (never the sum), and a rating at every icon up to its 320px fit ceiling, the way a date (Issue #159) and a toggle (Issue #586) already did. The chips are drawn by the cell's own renderer inside the column's own cell and measured there, so a CSS change moves the floor with it; they are measured again when an option is added, renamed or given an icon. The floor binds a drag, an Alt+arrow nudge, a double-click fit and the frozen-zone cap, and raises a stored width under it for that column only; a column with a long option now opens at that floor rather than the 124px default. A rating's floor supersedes Feature #235's "a width the reader dragged still wins" for widths under the icons. Free text still truncates with an ellipsis, and a checkbox column at its label floor already holds its whole box.
