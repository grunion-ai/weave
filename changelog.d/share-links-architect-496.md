- Share links are an architect's to mint, list, renew and revoke: a link passes the wall, so an editor no longer publishes a row, table, space or saved view by minting one, and cannot read back the token of a link an architect made. Every link now lasts thirty days unless an architect renews it on the same token (`weave share renew`, `weave_shares` renew, `POST /api/shares/<id>/renew`) or names its own expiry; links minted before expiry existed get thirty days from the upgrade (Issue #496).
