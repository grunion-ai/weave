- **A failed webhook or http-request is sent again** (Feature #311): a network error, a timeout, or an answer of 408, 429 or 5xx is retried after 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 and 60 minutes, eight attempts in all. While a delivery waits the Workflows record reads Warning, and Health Reason names the attempt and the next try; a retry that lands returns it to Healthy, and after the eighth attempt it reads Failed. The steps after a webhook now wait for its answer, as they already did after an http-request. The waiting delivery is stored in the workspace, so a restart loses nothing, and the minute schedule tick sends it. A retried webhook keeps its X-Weave-Delivery id. Gate: `test/delivery-retry.test.mjs`.
