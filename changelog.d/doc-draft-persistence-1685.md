- **An unsaved document draft survives the reload** (Issue #1685): a pending draft lived only in a module Map, so a refused save or a payload past the 60,000 byte keepalive ceiling died with the tab while the toast promised the draft was kept. Every scheduled save now writes the draft to local storage under its record and field, a landed save clears it, and reopening the record offers it back with Restore and Discard.
