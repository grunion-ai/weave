- **Swimlanes on a Side by side Board** (Feature #355): a second group level now draws as horizontal lanes across the columns instead of being ignored. Each lane heading sticks to the left edge and folds through the view's collapsed group paths, so the fold matches Stacked and List. Dragging a card into another lane writes the second group field and into another column the first, with Undo. Empty lanes follow empty groups as columns do, and a search keeps only the lanes that hold a card. Stacked keeps its nested sections. On a phone the columns snap at 85vw under sticky headings and the lane fold is a 44 px target. Gates: `test/board-swimlanes.test.mjs`, `test/board-swimlanes-browser.test.mjs`.
