- **Rich cells: a colour setting, redrawn cells and fitted column widths** (Feature #235): Kyle, 2026-09-25, on the bars, rings, heat, ratings and sparklines of Features #230 to #232: "I don't like the design of the columns or the colors." His decision the next day: put colour in the field's config, default to quiet ink, redraw the cells to the A/B/C mockups, and give the columns better widths. A rating, a number's display and a formula's display now take `color`: `ink` (Quiet ink, the default, never written down), `icon` (Color by icon) or `accent` (One accent hue). Quiet ink draws bars and rings in the text colour on a hairline track, heat as a grey tint that deepens with the value, rating icons filled in the text colour with hairline outlines for the empties, and sparklines in the secondary text colour. Color by icon draws bars and rings teal, runs heat from cool to warm with the share, fills a star amber, a heart rose and a bolt violet (any other icon takes the accent), draws the sparkline blue and win/loss green and red. One accent hue draws every graphic in the workspace accent, losses in a lighter accent. Every colour is a token with a dark twin. The engine validates the key on add, update and schema apply, and refuses anything else with the list; the schema says the colour of every field that draws a graphic, ink included; a lookup and a rollup draw in the colour of the column they read, and the chip and the card carry it. The cells are redrawn: a 6px bar with round ends on a fixed 80px track and the figure to its right in tabular figures, a 16px ring with a 2.5px round-capped stroke hugging its percent, heat as a rounded tint behind the figure, 14px rating icons 1px apart, and an 80 by 18 sparkline drawn at its own size with a dot on the newest point, 1.5px column corners and small win/loss blocks either side of a midline. A rich cell no longer repeats the Σ or ƒ its header carries. A bar, ring, heat or sparkline column opens at the width its graphic needs (a bar's track plus its widest figure, a ring or a heat tint plus its widest figure, a sparkline's 80px), never under a plain number's 88px; a width a person drags wins. A rating column keeps the width rule Issue #404 landed, measured on the new 14px icon, and a rating column dragged narrower than its icons, or past the fit cap, draws a compact `★ 7/10` instead of cutting icons off. The settings tray gains a Color picker beside Display on a rating, a number display and a sparkline: three swatches, each this field's own graphic drawn in that setting, and the Sample and the rating's default row wear the pick. The Showcase's Field Types table gains a column per setting beside the ink ones (Momentum, Reach, Warmth, Stars, Bolts, Hearts, Age line, Delta blocks), through the existing Showcase sync.
