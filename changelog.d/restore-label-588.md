- **A restored document revision says it is a restore** (Issue #588): restoring a revision used to read as an ordinary edit ("Current · web · −79", a plain `doc-updated`), and its Activity entry could fold into the edits around it under another person's name. The new revision now stores `restoredFrom` (the seq it brought back; a `restored_from` column added to `doc_revisions` on open), its history row reads "Restored from 5 min ago" with the version's date and author on hover, and its Activity entry is its own row reading "Description restored to the version from 10/5/2026, 9:49 PM by bob" with `restoredFrom`, `restoredAt` and `restoredBy` in its detail. Typing straight after a restore starts a new revision and a new entry, so the restore keeps the text it restored. Tests in `test/doc-revisions.test.mjs` and `test/doc-history-browser.test.mjs`.
