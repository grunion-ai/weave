- **The workspace logo's type comes from its bytes** (Issue #492): `PUT /api/workspace/logo` stored the content type the writer named and `GET` served it back, so a writer could plant an HTML page at the logo URL. Upload now takes PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP (proved by their leading bytes) and SVG (the chip draws the logo through an `<img>`, and the logo picker and its browser test use SVG), and answers anything else with 415. The logo is served with the type its bytes prove, `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` and `Content-Security-Policy: sandbox; default-src 'none'`; a logo stored earlier whose bytes are not an image downloads as `application/octet-stream`. Gate: `test/security/logo-type.test.mjs`.
