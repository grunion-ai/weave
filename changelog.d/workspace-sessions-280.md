- Each workspace keeps its own browser session, in a cookie named `wv_session_<workspace id>`. Signing out of one workspace ends only that workspace's session, idle expiry of one leaves the others signed in, and **Sign out of every workspace** on `/auth` (`POST /api/auth/logout?everywhere=1`) ends them all. Removing an account ends its sessions in that workspace at once, and the next visit there answers a page saying access was removed instead of a sign-in redirect. A plain `wv_session` cookie from before this change is still read for one release and moves to the new name on first use; a session on the hub root still opens member workspaces until hub root removal lands (Feature #280).
