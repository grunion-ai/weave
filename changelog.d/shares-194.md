- **Share links: one grant for a row, table, space, saved view or the whole workspace** (Feature #194). A grant carries a `wvs_` token, a scope, an access rung (read, edit, manage; comment is reserved and answers 403), a public or private visibility, a label, an optional expiry and a revoke stamp. `/s/<token>` opens read-only pages for its scope: a row's fields and documents, a table's rows under the columns its schema names, a space's tables, a view's blocks. `Authorization: Bearer wvs_…` reaches the scoped JSON API, where edit changes rows in scope and manage also adds and trashes them; no link touches schema or the registry, and anything outside the scope answers 404. One `withinScope` check gates the pages, the reads and the writes. A private link opens only for a signed-in account. Editors and architects mint from the **Share…** dialog on a row, table, space or saved view, `weave share`, `weave_shares` or `/api/shares`; an architect revokes any link, an editor its own, and every mint and revoke is audited. The export leaves tokens out. Saved-view share tokens lift into grants on open, and `/view/<token>` now redirects to `/s/<token>`, so links already handed out keep working.
