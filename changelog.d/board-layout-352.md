- **Board layout: kanban columns and a picture gallery in one table layout** (Feature #352): a view takes `layout: 'board'` beside Table and List, with `groups` (`stacked` sections or `side` columns, default `side`) and `picture` (`off`, `small`, `medium` or `large`, default the cover field's size; cards 132/188/256 px, columns 184/232/288 px, three small cards per row on a phone). The picture is the record's cover, the first shown attachments field set to Show as cover, through one `coverOf(db, entity)` in `public/board-core.js` that the record page now shares. The View menu shows Board with Columns and Gallery presets and Groups and Picture controls. Cards drag with `RO().sortable`: into another group sets its value with Undo, within a group or with no groups saves the view's order in reading order, and a sort turns hand order off with a Manual button in the footer. Card fields edit through the table cell editor. A click anywhere on a card (cover, title, a field label, blank space) opens the record in the pane row with the open card outlined, and a click on a field value also opens that field's editor; a press that moves is a drag. On a phone (600 px and under) Stacked shows 3 small or 2 larger cards per row, Side by side snaps 85vw columns under sticky headings, a 250 ms hold lifts a card while a swipe scrolls, a lifted card auto-scrolls on both axes, every card target is 44 px tall, and a tap anywhere on a card opens the full-screen sheet. A query that names an attachments field now returns that field's files. `weave_table_view`, `weave table view --groups --picture`, the schema document and the Views registry (Groups, Picture) carry the new keys. Gates: `test/board-layout.test.mjs`, `test/board-layout-browser.test.mjs`, `test/board-layout-phone-browser.test.mjs` (Chromium at 375 × 812 and WebKit iPhone 15).
