- **The window stops scrolling; each pane scrolls on its own** (Issue #609): `#app` was `min-height: 100vh` and `#main` grew to fit its content, so a long table or record scrolled the whole document, and the workspace rail and left nav held still only through `position: sticky; height: 100vh`. The dock was a sticky box with a scroller of its own, so it scrolled alone while the main panel scrolled with the page. The shell is now a fixed frame: the document never scrolls, and the rail, the left nav, the main panel and the dock each own one scroller; a scroll that runs out at a pane's edge stops there, since the only thing it could chain to is the document. Every reader of the window's scroll now reads its pane: the grid's row window and its page fetches, the wide grid's height cut, the grid loader's placement, the doc rail's reading line, and the sticky offsets of the view header, the field headers, the Σ row, the section heads and the rail, which share one `--wv-pane-pad` term. A stuck `+ New` foot hangs a strip of the surface over the panel's bottom padding, so rows no longer show through under it. The 600px phone dock (fixed, full screen) and the nav drawer keep working.
