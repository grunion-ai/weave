- A lookup or a rollup repoints in place. Its settings tray offers the Relation, Target field and Aggregate pickers a create offers; save, and the column keeps its name, description, width, views and position while every cell reads the new recipe. `updateField`, `PATCH /api/tables/:id/fields/:id`, `weave field update --config` and `weave_update_field` take `relationField`, `targetField` and `aggregate`, checked by the same recipe check `addField` runs, so an edit is refused exactly where a create is. A new relation keeps the old target field only when its far table has it. The tray warns when the result changes kind, the answer lists under `formulas` every formula of the table that now fails on the column, and the Activity entry names the old and the new recipe so Undo puts it back. A space rollup keeps its recipe read-only. The formula scan now counts a NaN result as an error. (Feature #241)
