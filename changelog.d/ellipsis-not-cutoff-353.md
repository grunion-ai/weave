- The cut-off gate no longer counts an ellipsis as cut-off text. A label that ends in "…" or a clamped paragraph that ends on a whole line draws every letter it shows, so the gate now fails only on letters sliced by a box edge: a line cut through its middle, a chip cut at its cell's edge, or a clamp whose box is shorter than its lines. The baseline drops its 50 ellipsis and line-clamp instances per platform. (Feature #353)
