- weave now keeps the verified email address from sign-in. It asks the provider for `openid email`, stores one address per sign-in identity only when the provider marks it verified, and stores no name, picture or other profile claim. This reverses Feature #252 for the email claim only. After sign-in, `/start` lists the invites waiting for that address next to the person's workspaces and accepts one in place; the one-time invite link still works. Architects see each member's address in Members, a person sees their own at `/api/auth/me`, a later sign-in fills it in for an identity linked before this release, deleting the account deletes it, and `weave export` leaves it out. The privacy policy says so (Feature #290).
- `test/verified-email.test.mjs` covers the verified-only rule, normalisation, backfill, export and delete, single-use acceptance by the invited address only, the start page rows and the Members view; `test/auth-oidc.test.mjs` now expects the `openid email` scope (Feature #290).
