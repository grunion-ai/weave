- Spaces are open or private. Open stays the default, so a one-person workspace sees no change. A private space shows itself to its members and the workspace architects only: anyone else gets a 404 on its tables and rows and finds nothing of it in search, the space and table lists, the schema, the ontology, the System registry rows, views, MCP tools, CSV and markdown exports, share links and the audit log. A relation from an open space into a private one shows a locked chip named "Private", and lookups, rollups and formulas leave the private rows out. A member may carry a role for that space that overrides the workspace role, so an Observer can edit in Sales alone, and a space Editor takes a paid seat. Workspace architects and the space's own architects manage its members (`PUT` and `DELETE /api/spaces/<space>/members/<account>`, `weave_update_space` with `access`, `member`, `role` and `removeMember`). An agent token can be scoped to one space (`createAccount` with `space`), never reaches past the account that issued it, and writes an audit entry for every change it makes. A full JSON export needs access to every space (Feature #141).
