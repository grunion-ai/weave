- **Failed Deliveries, replayable** (Feature #311): a webhook or http-request that runs out of attempts becomes a record of the new built-in `System/Failed Deliveries` table, holding the rule, the record that fired it, the request with any credential held by name only, each attempt and the last error. Setting its Status to Replay, `weave automation replay <record>`, `POST /api/automations/deliveries/<record>/replay` or `weave_automations` with `action: replay` sends it again and runs the steps after it; the record then reads Delivered and the rule Healthy. Gate: `test/delivery-dead-letter.test.mjs`.
