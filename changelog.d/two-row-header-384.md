- **Two-row header** (Feature #384): row 1 holds the breadcrumb, Copy permalink and the pane controls (Expand and Close in the dock, Close on a record page). Row 2 holds the table's icon, title and description toggle with Search, View, Share, the table menu and a new **New** button named for the table's record ("New bug"), on table and board views; on a record, row 2 holds its table icon and `#id` with Show or hide fields, Share and the record menu. A record's breadcrumb ends at the table and the `#id`, so its name shows once, as the title (Issue #1862). The View button keeps its count of active filters in row 2 (Issue #1863), and the title, description toggle and toolbar share one centred line with no dead band above the grid (Issue #1867). Table titles drop to 16 px at weight 600 and record titles to 20 px.
