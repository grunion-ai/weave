- **A schedule saved just before its due minute fires on that minute** (Issue #1609): a never-run schedule rule used to open its window at the first minute tick that saw it, so a `0 9 * * *` rule saved at 08:59:50 and ticked at 09:00:30 skipped that day. The write that creates, edits or switches the rule On now opens the window. A never-run rule the server first sees after a restart still starts at that tick, and the synchronous fan-out over every matching row is unchanged.
