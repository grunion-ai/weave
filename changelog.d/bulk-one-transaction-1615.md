- CSV imports, bulk edits and bulk moves write in one transaction, and weave no longer keeps the full-text index that nothing read. Each saved row deleted its old `entities_fts` entry by an unindexed id, a scan of the whole index, and each imported, edited or moved row committed to disk on its own. Importing 500 CSV rows into a table of 8,000 rows with documents took 1.3 s on an SSD and now takes 55 ms. Search still matches names, text values, documents and comments. Opening a workspace drops the old index and its tables (Issue #1615).
