- **Templates gallery** (Feature #244): a Templates link in the sidebar, under + New space, opens `#/templates`, a gallery of every starter grouped by category. An architect picks one to add its space of tables and sample records to the workspace already in use, and lands on its first table. A starter whose space the workspace already holds shows "In this workspace" and opens that space instead of building it twice. Editors and observers see the gallery without the build. The page loads `public/starter-gallery.js` on demand. Gate: `test/starter-gallery-browser.test.mjs` (both themes, 375 px).
