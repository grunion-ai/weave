- **Keys answer to the hub root's accounts, whichever workspace the URL names** (Issue #480): one keystore serves every workspace of a process, but `/api/keys` and reveal were gated by the accounts of the workspace in the URL, so a member workspace with no accounts opened every key. Once the hub root has an account, managing keys needs an authenticated root admin; a root caller on a key's access list may reveal it, and a key's owner may rotate or delete it. The engine's reveal check reads the root's accounts too. An instance with no accounts anywhere works as before. Gate: `test/security/keystore-root-gate.test.mjs`.
