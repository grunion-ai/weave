- **Credential access lists hold account ids only** (Issue #497): a key's owner and its grants are stored and compared as `account:<id>`, so a caller whose actor is only a name, such as the CLI's user name, no longer opens, shares or rotates a credential owned by the account of that name. Setting a key records the caller's account id as owner, or none when the caller has no account. Sharing or unsharing a key with no account owner (one from the original keystore, or one whose owner is a bare name) needs an architect on the hub root, and sharing no longer makes the sharer its owner. A grant to a name with no account is refused. Re-grant a legacy credential with `weave key share <name> --with <account>` (or `POST /api/keys/<name>/share`) as a root architect.
