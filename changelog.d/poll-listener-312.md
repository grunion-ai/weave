- **Automations fire when a polled source changes** (Feature #312): a `listener-changed` trigger on a Workflows record reads a list endpoint through a System/Integrations key on the minute tick, compares the answer with the stored snapshot and runs the rule once per new or changed item, with the item as `trigger.item`. The first poll is the baseline and fires nothing. A cursor and an ETag go back to the source, a change weave itself just sent is an echo and does not fire, an unchanged source is polled less often down to the Script's `maxInterval`, the new integration column Poll Budget caps the polls an hour across rules, and a 429 waits for its Retry-After with the rule in Warning. Health Reason shows the last poll, the next poll and the items seen. The snapshot and cursor survive a restart, and the key is never stored with them. Gate: `test/automation-listener.test.mjs`.
