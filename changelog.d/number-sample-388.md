- **The number Sample draws the column's own figures** (Issue #388): Kyle, reading Showcase > Field Types > Progress, "I don't understand the config for this rollup or formula driven rating; config tray looks wrong." The column holds 72, 100 and 15; its settings tray drew three bars labelled 0.25, 0.6 and 1, fixed placeholders that matched no row and no format, so a scale of "column max" could not be judged from the tray at all. The Sample now draws this column's own smallest, middle and largest figures, dressed in the costume the form holds and measured against the scale it holds: 15, 72 and 100 against a column max of 100, and the same three figures against a fixed 200 when the scale moves. Equal figures collapse, so a one-row column draws one bar rather than three of the same. A column holding no numbers yet keeps the quarter, three fifths and whole of the scale, in the costume's own range (0..1 under percent, 0..100 otherwise), under the note "Example figures. This column holds no numbers yet." The figures come from `GET /api/tables/:ref/stats`, the same five-number summary the rollup row reads, fetched once per dialog and only when a graphic display asks for it; a field being created has no column and samples examples. The Sample stays empty until the column answers, so a figure the field never holds is never painted, not even for a frame. The number costume moved out of `src/engine.js` into `public/number-core.js`, the classic-script-plus-ESM pattern `cell-graphics.js` uses, so the tray dresses a figure through the very function the cell dresses its value with.
