- Spaces, tables and the workspaces in the rail keep a stored order. A `position` (0 is the first place) moves a space or a table on every door: `weave_update_space`, `weave_update_table` and `weave_move_table`, `PATCH /api/spaces/:space`, `PATCH /api/tables/:table` and `POST /api/tables/:table/move`, and `weave space update`, `weave table update` and `weave table move` with `--position N`. The workspaces' order lives on the hub root: `weave_workspace` order and move, `PATCH /api/workspaces/:name`, and `weave workspace order` and `weave workspace move`. A workspace saved before this keeps the order it showed, and a moved table lands last in its new space (Feature #284).
