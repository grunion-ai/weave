- **A workspace link signs in on the setup page** (Issue #994): with `WEAVE_BASE_DOMAIN` set, a signed-out visit to a path-mounted workspace link such as `/w/weave/e/1` used to go from `/w/weave/auth` straight to the identity provider, which showed Clerk's plain hosted sign-in page, and its Sign up link skipped onboarding. The workspace `/auth` now answers `302 /start?next=<workspace path>`; `/start` keeps a `next` only when it is a same-origin `/w/<workspace>/` path. The in-pane Clerk sign-in then goes on through that workspace's sign-in and lands on the linked page, a sign-up goes to the setup steps, and a visitor already signed in on `/start` who opens that workspace goes straight on. `/w/<workspace>/auth?signed-out=1`, workspace hosts, invite links and deployments without a base domain keep their sign-in as before. Gates: `test/setup-routes.test.mjs` and `test/workspace-signin-start-browser.test.mjs`.
