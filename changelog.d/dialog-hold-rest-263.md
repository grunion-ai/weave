- **The tray, the full-screen viewer and the ⌘K palette hold the page too** (Issue #263): the first half gave `modal()` a hold; the other three surfaces that cover the page still let Tab walk out into the grid behind, left it clickable to a keyboard, and dropped focus on the body when they closed. `holdPage()` in `public/app.js` now takes the element or text that names the dialog (the viewer's title, "Search" for the palette) and holds stack: each hold keeps the list of what it made inert, and when one lets go that list passes to the hold still on top, except the top hold's own backdrop, which comes back to life. So `tray()` replacing a modal keeps the page held (the modal's late release used to free the page from under the tray), and ⌘K, which opens over a dialog as it always has, holds that dialog too and hands it back on close. The field tray is a labelled dialog whose pickers are appended after the hold and stay live, and its Escape still waits for an open picker. The viewer is the dialog box itself: it takes focus on open, its frame is a Tab stop, and a stop after the frame catches a Tab leaving the framed page's last control and sends it to the toolbar's first. The palette's combobox keeps its own arrows and Escape; it frees the page before a caller's `onPick` or `onDismiss` runs, so a reference command's cursor lands back in its editor. Shift+Enter (quick-create) now also stands down while the tray or the viewer is open: a type tile focused in the tray used to create a row behind it. Unchanged: Escape in the palette over a dialog closes both, as before. Gates: `test/dialog-hold-browser.test.mjs` (new, 15 cases: per surface, role, `aria-modal` and name, Tab and Shift+Tab wrap, `#app` inert while open and free after Escape and after the surface's own close, focus back to the opener; a picker inside the tray stays live; the tray replacing a modal; the viewer's frame in the Tab cycle; ⌘K over a modal; a palette pick refocusing its editor; light and dark).
