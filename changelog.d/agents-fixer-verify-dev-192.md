- `node agents/fixer/verify-dev.mjs <sha>` waits for dev.weave.grunion.ai to serve a merged commit. It polls the anonymous `/api/health` every 10 seconds, accepts a full sha or a prefix of 7 or more characters, refuses a commit whose last swap failed, and exits 1 after `--timeout` seconds (default 900) with the last health body on stderr. The cloud fixer uses it in place of the Mac's `:4400` read copy, which is locked (Feature #192).
