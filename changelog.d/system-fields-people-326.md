- Created At and Modified At read like date fields ("Aug 28, 2026 5:14 PM" in your own zone), with seconds and the zone on hover. Created By and Modified By chips come from the Workspace/Users table: a person shows initials, an agent a glyph, the chip opens the Users row and its hover gives Name, Role and Email. An actor with no Users row, such as `web`, is a quiet chip with no link (Feature #326).
