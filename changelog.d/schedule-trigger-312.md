- **Automations fire on a schedule** (Feature #312): a Workflows row's Script takes `trigger: {"type": "schedule", "cron": "0 9 * * 1-5", "zone": "America/Los_Angeles", "where": [["State", "!=", "Done"]]}`. The five-field cron expression is read on the zone's wall clock (UTC when no zone is given), and at each due minute the rule runs its actions on every live row of its table, or on the rows the `where` filter admits, as the automation actor, stamping Health and Last Run per run. The server ticks every workspace once a minute (`src/schedule.js`); a due minute fires once, a box that slept catches up with one run going back seven days at most, a new rule starts at the first tick that sees it, and a rule switched On after its minute waits for the next one. A bad cron, zone or where clause reads Setup incomplete with the reason. REST, MCP (`weave_create_automation`) and the CLI take the trigger as they take the others; the relation map pill reads `cron 0 9 * * 1-5`. Gate: `test/automation-schedule.test.mjs`.
