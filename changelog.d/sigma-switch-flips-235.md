- **A Σ switch flips on the click** (Issue #235): Kyle filed "visibility toggles are slow to switch" from a uno table, and the trace's clicks were in the Σ row's picker. Each switch waited on its POST, then on `GET /stats` (every column summarised and every rollup of the table resolved, to repaint one cell), then on a schema reload, 1 to 1.5 s a click. A switch now flips when you click it. Its write waits its turn on the eye's write queue (Issue #243) and decides at that turn whether there is anything to create or delete, so a switch flicked on and off before the first write answers ends off, with no duplicate field and no conflict toast. The tab adds or drops the one Spaces field itself, in the shape `describeSchema()` gives it (a rollup over a bar, ring or heat column carries that `display` and a fixed `scale`, one over a rating carries its `rating`), so no schema reload happens, a later refocus finds nothing changed, and the grid is not redrawn. The last flip of a burst reads back only its column (`GET /api/tables/:ref/stats?field=F`, with `field` on `weave_stats` and `--field` on `weave stats`) and repaints that one footer cell. A write that fails toasts and puts its switch back. The read-back was still dominated by dressing: a space rollup dressed every row's value although only `distinct` and `join` read the dressed list, and the stats report did the same for number and date columns, and a currency builds an `Intl.NumberFormat` per row. Measured in the page on a 2,000-row, 18-field table: the switch moved 165 to 375 ms after the click and now moves within 1 ms; the footer figure, which used to land with the switch, lands 18 to 65 ms after the click; the full `/stats` read dropped from 195 to 40 ms, and one column's read-back takes 7 ms. Gates: `test/footer-rollups-browser.test.mjs` gains "a Σ switch flips before its write answers, and only its column is read back" (holds the read-back open, asserts the switch is on, then counts one POST and one narrow stats read, no `/schema`, no row query, the same grid body, and a refocus that finds nothing to redraw), "a Σ switch flicked on and off before its write answers ends off, with one write each", "a Σ write that fails puts the switch back and says why", and "a Σ over a bar column, flipped on, leaves a refocus nothing to redraw" with its rating twin; `test/space-rollups.test.mjs` gains "tableStats narrowed to one field summarises that column and only the rollups under it" and "REST, MCP and CLI carry the stats field narrowing".
