- **Workspace display names and case-blind slugs** (Issues #592, #599): a workspace now carries a title a person reads beside the slug its URL answers at. Renaming to "Personal finance" (REST `PATCH /api/workspace`, `weave_workspace action:update`, the CLI, the onboarding welcome) keeps that text as `title` and moves the workspace to `/w/personal-finance/`, where the old rule refused anything but letters, digits, `-` and `_`. A name with nothing usable left is refused with the rule spelled out. Slugs are case-blind, like Slack, Notion and GitHub: `/w/ACME/` opens `acme`, and creating or renaming to `Acme` while `acme` exists answers 409 instead of making a second workspace file on a case-sensitive volume. Existing workspace files keep their names and their mixed-case slugs still resolve. `weave_workspace` over HTTP now renames through the hub as REST does, so the new slug resolves at once and a held one is refused.
