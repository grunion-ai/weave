- **Undo after moving a table, space or share page to the trash restores it in place** (Issue #856): `trashRow` reopened the object on Undo whenever it had been open at delete time, so a reader who had since moved to another page was pulled back to it. The trash row now records the page the delete left the reader on. Undo reopens the object only when the reader is still on that page; otherwise it restores, leaves the current page alone and shows `Restored` with an Open action. Gate: `test/menu-trash-browser.test.mjs` (3 new cases: a table, a space and a share page trashed while open, then the reader opens another table; Undo keeps that table's hash and Open in the toast goes to the restored object).
