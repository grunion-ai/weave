- **An attachments field says how the record shows its files** (Feature #266; Kyle, 2026-10-05): three config keys on the field, through `normalizeSelfContainedConfig`, the field tray, `describeSchema` and `applySchema`. `preview` is `link` (chips), `inline` (a contact sheet of the files, or the one file in its viewer), `auto` (pictures in the sheet, the rest as chips) or `cover` (the picture above the record; single-file fields only); unset follows the field, `auto` for many and `inline` for one. `size` (`small`, `medium`, `large`) is the sheet's cell and the viewer's height; `fit` is `trim` (the whole file, the cell trimmed to its shape) or `fill` (uniform cells, the file cropped). Nothing is stretched. A sheet cell opens the fullscreen viewer, which gained ← → between a field's files and a sandbox for HTML. A single-file field shows its file in place with no click: an image, a PDF or plain text in a frame, an HTML upload live under the new `GET /api/files/:id?view`, served `text/html` only under `HTML_VIEW_POLICY` (sandboxed with scripts, never same-origin, nothing remote) and framed with the same sandbox; the bare route still downloads it (Issue #483 holds). Gates: `test/attachments-preview.test.mjs`, `test/attachments-preview-browser.test.mjs`.
