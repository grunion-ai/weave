- **A numeric-string offset or limit pages as a number on every door** (Issue #1725): the engine now reads a query's `offset` and `limit` as whole numbers whether they arrive as numbers or numeric strings, so REST `offset: "1"` with `limit: 5` answers 5 rows instead of 14. Anything else (`"x"`, `2.5`, `true`, an empty string, a negative offset) is refused as `invalid` with `offset must be a whole number of rows, not 'x'`, over REST, MCP, the CLI and the engine alike. A negative limit still reads the whole table over REST.
