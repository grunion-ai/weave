- **The workspace rail is up at first paint** (Issue #1283): switching workspaces is a document navigation, and the rail and the wordmark used to wait for `GET /api/workspaces`, so the shell painted with an empty rail and no workspace name and both popped in tens to hundreds of milliseconds later. A successful answer now caches what it drew, `/ws-boot.js` runs before first paint and redraws it from that cache, and the answer reconciles: a workspace the server no longer lists leaves the rail and the chips that stay keep their place. A first visit, and a URL the cache does not name, paint no chip rather than a wrong one.
