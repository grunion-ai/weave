- **A density flip on a page-scrolled grid lands on the reader's row** (Issue #413): on a page-scrolled grid of uneven rows, Comfortable to Compact put row 224 at the top of the view where row 223 had been (600 rows, 2187x1359, standing at 10,800px, both themes). The flip from Issue #342 put the row back under the header once, and the re-window after that correction read it as travel: the buffer turned to the other side of the window, and the spacer that replaced those rows did not weigh what they did, so the row moved again (Issue #317). `wrap.wvSetDensity` now re-anchors the direction of travel before each corrective re-window and reads the row again, up to three passes, until it holds. The header's edge also keeps the fraction of the row it cut before the flip. The offset math is ported from the dropped `wvKeepPlace` in e6c63dd. Gates: `test/grid-density-browser.test.mjs` (2 new cases: a page-scrolled grid of uneven rows keeps the row heading the view across Compact and back to Comfortable, in light and dark; both failed on the parent at 223 to 224).
