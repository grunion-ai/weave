- Undo, bulk writes, pasted and filled ranges, workflow state moves and relation edits now patch the rows they changed instead of refetching the page and redrawing the grid (Issue #1693). `POST /api/undo`, `POST /api/bulk`, `/state`, `/link` and `/unlink` answer with `affected`, the rows the write changed, the way `PATCH /api/entities/:id` already did, and the grid carries that list through its refresh paths. A view that sorts or filters, a grouped view, a `move` or `rollup` bulk, and an undo that puts a row back or takes one away still redraw, since patching cannot place a row that moves.
