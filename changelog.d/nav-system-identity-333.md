- The foot of the left nav is redesigned. Workflows, Users, Activity and Trash live in a built-in System space pinned under your spaces, with no counts; it folds like any space and remembers the fold. Under it, an identity panel shows your avatar, name and your role in this workspace as a coloured chip, and its menu holds Theme, Keyboard shortcuts and Sign out with the version beside it. The rail loses its ? and theme buttons; the ? key still opens the shortcuts. The rows and size line, the uptime and the version chip are gone; a stale or behind server still warns. (Feature #333)
- The stored system space is named System (its key stays `workspace`), so qualified names read `System/Users`, `System/Spaces` and so on; every workspace is renamed on open, and `Workspace/...` names keep resolving. (Feature #333)
