- A hosted weave instance now logs the layout shifts and long animation frames its users hit. `public/paint.js` watches `layout-shift` (input-led shifts skipped) and, where the browser supports it, `long-animation-frame`; a shift session over 0.1 or a frame of 200 ms or more goes to `POST /api/paint` in a throttled beacon, at most one every ten seconds. The server writes one `weave: paint {json}` line per report to its log: the score and px move, the selector of the largest moved element, the route with workspace names and ids stripped, viewport, engine family and build. No field values, names or emails reach it. The endpoint takes 8 KB at most, six reports a minute from one address and 600 a minute in all, and answers 404 on a local install (no `WEAVE_ORIGIN`). The privacy page names the report ([[Issue#1553]]).
