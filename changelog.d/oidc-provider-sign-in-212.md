- **Sign in with an OpenID Connect provider** (Feature #212, door C of Feature #222): with `WEAVE_OIDC_ISSUER` and `WEAVE_OIDC_CLIENT_ID` set, the sign-in page at `/auth` carries a link to one provider (Clerk, Auth0, Keycloak, Authentik, Google), and a person who signs in there comes back with the same `wv_session` cookie a passkey mints. The flow is authorization code with PKCE at `GET /api/auth/oidc/start` and `GET /api/auth/oidc/callback`; the id token's signature (RS256 or ES256), issuer, audience, expiry and nonce are verified in `src/oidc.js` with no dependency. A sign-in finishes only in the browser that started it: start sets a five-minute `wv_oidc` cookie and the callback refuses a request without it. Signing in creates no account: `weave account link <name> --email <address>` (or `POST /api/accounts/<name>/identities`, or `weave_accounts` `action: link-identity`) opens an account to the identity with that verified email, and the first sign-in pins the provider's subject to it. `WEAVE_OIDC_CLIENT_SECRET` and `WEAVE_OIDC_NAME` are optional. Gate: `test/auth-oidc.test.mjs`.
