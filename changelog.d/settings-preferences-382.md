- Settings is one panel with two pages. Preferences holds your theme, the day your week starts, a date format and a time format for date fields that set none, and a start page that reopens the last page you visited when you arrive from a bookmark, a typed address or another site. Workspace holds link preview and workspace deletion. Preferences stay in your browser. Someone signed in through Clerk sees Settings as Clerk's account profile, with Preferences and Workspace as pages inside it. Delete forever moved from Settings to each row of the Trash page, behind the same typed name and hold (Feature #382).
