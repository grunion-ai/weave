- The table and the record open the same Fields menu. The eye button on a record used to open a second, smaller menu of switches with no reorder, while the table's opened a checkbox list with grips; one builder now draws both. Every row is a switch with a grip on its left, Show all, Hide all and New field sit on both, and dragging a row or pressing ↑ / ↓ on its grip reorders the view's columns on a table and the table's field order on a record. The Deleted and Σ rollup row switches stay on the table menu only. The record menu opens under its own button, in the dock as on the full page (Issue #910).
- The Fields list keeps its switches clear of the scrollbar. The list reserves its scrollbar gutter whether or not it scrolls and pads its right edge to match the grip's inset on the left, so a switch sits 8 to 12px from the scroll thumb and no row shifts when the list starts scrolling (Issue #909).
