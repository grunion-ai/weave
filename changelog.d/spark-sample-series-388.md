The Sample now draws the list on the first row that holds two numbers or more, in the chosen style and colour, under a line naming that row ("Acme: 4 values, oldest first"), and each Color swatch draws the same series. A column with no list yet, and a field being created, draw an example series under "Example series. No row of this column holds a list yet." Nothing is drawn until the rows answer, so an example never flashes over a real series. With Feature #235 the number Sample wears the chosen colour as well as the format, scale and display.
