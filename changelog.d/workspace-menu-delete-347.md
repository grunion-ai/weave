- **Delete workspace… is in the workspace menu, and it is permanent** (Feature #347): the ⋮ beside the workspace title and a right-click on a rail chip (or a click on the current one) open the same menu, and its one row opens the **Delete workspace** dialog. The dialog says the delete cannot be undone and arms only on the exact name. The workspace leaves the rail for everyone, its address answers 404, and there is no Undo and no Trash entry; the hub keeps a tombstone only the operator can restore. The row shows only where you may delete that workspace, so the default workspace has no menu. Deleting the workspace you are in opens the next workspace on the rail; deleting your last one lands you on the weave workspace, which a sign-in whose only workspace was deleted still reads as an Observer.
- **Invite people… and Link preview before sign-in left the workspace menu** (Feature #347): invite from the **Invite** button beside the workspace title, and turn the link preview on or off with its switch on the **Settings** page (or `weave workspace link-preview`).
