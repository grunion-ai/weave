- **Delete workspace… is back in the workspace menu** (Feature #347): the ⋮ beside the workspace title and a right-click on a rail chip (or a click on the current one) open the same menu, and its one row opens the **Delete workspace** dialog that arms on the exact name and sends `DELETE /api/workspaces/:id`. The row shows only where you may delete that workspace, so the default workspace has no menu.
