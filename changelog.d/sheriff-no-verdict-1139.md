- The sheriff no longer reverts a pull request when its rerun is cancelled, timed out, skipped or never reaches its `CI gate` job. Only a `success` or `failure` gate decides guilt; anything else is rerun once more and then handed to the next red main run. It also re-checks that main has not gone green on its own immediately before it opens the revert, and a revert whose range took no bisect probe names that in its subject. (Issue #1139)
