- **Signing out of a workspace opened through the hub root leaves the root signed in** (Issue #716): a member workspace with no account for you opens on the root workspace's session, so `POST /w/<member>/api/auth/logout` revoked that root session and signed the browser out of the root and of every other workspace the fallthrough opened. That broke the Feature #280 promise that signing out of one workspace touches no other. The logout route now revokes only a session the workspace owns. For a borrowed session it sets an `HttpOnly` `wv_out_<workspace id>` cookie instead, which stops the fallthrough for that workspace in this browser; signing in to that workspace again clears it, and Sign out everywhere clears it with the sessions. Gate: `test/auth-workspace-sessions.test.mjs` (1 new case: after a member sign-out the root answers 200 with its session intact, the member answers 401, another member still opens, a fresh sign-in reopens the member, and Sign out everywhere ends the root session and clears the marker).
