- **One Activity panel for the whole record, with Restore on the revision** (Issue #429): the document history was a cramped list inside the section ("3 REVISIONS Select one to view it · Esc closes", the hint clipped at the right edge) whose rows offered no revert; Restore sat behind a second click, on a preview with no diff. Now the clock-arrow in a record's header opens a panel beside it with every document's revisions, field and state changes, and comments, newest first under Today, Yesterday and a date, and a document's own clock-arrow opens the same panel filtered to that document. Rows are nav rows: what changed on one line, who and when on the next, and the row's action (Restore, Revert, Delete) one click away on hover. A selected row carries one button and, for a revision, a picker to compare it with any other version of that document; the change shows read-only where it lives, a document's diff in place of its editor (blocks, then sentences, then words, unchanged runs folded) and a field change as a card at the top. Every action toasts with Undo in the corner lane, and Undo steps back that action only, refusing if something else was written since. A restore's row reads "Description restored from <time>" (Issue #588). Gates: `test/history-core.test.mjs`, `test/doc-history-browser.test.mjs`.
