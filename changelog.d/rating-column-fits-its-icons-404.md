- **A rating column opens wide enough for its own icons** (Issue #404): every rating column opened at the type default of 104px whatever its `max`, so the showcase's Love (max 7, 132px of icons) and Brightness (max 12, 227px) drew their last hearts and suns cut at the cell edge: a 7 of 7 read like a 5 of 7, and the right-most icon could not be clicked. `defaultWidth` in `public/column-resize.js` now opens a rating at `max` icons plus the gaps between them plus the cell's own horizontal padding, floored at the 104px type default and capped at the 320px fit ceiling (Feature #235's column-width rule). The icon box and the gap are constants held to what the browser paints; the padding is measured off the rendered cell, because Tabler gives the row's last cell 20px on the right where every other cell gets 4. Only the default moves: a width the reader dragged still wins. Gates: `test/column-layout.test.mjs`, `test/rating-browser.test.mjs`.
