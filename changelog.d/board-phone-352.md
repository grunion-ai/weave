- **Board layout opens on any click and fits a phone** (Feature #352): a click anywhere on a card (cover, title, a field label, blank space) opens the record in the pane row, and a click on a field value also opens that field's editor; a press that moves is a drag. On a phone (600 px and under) Stacked shows 3 small or 2 larger cards per row, Side by side snaps 85vw columns under sticky headings, a 250 ms hold lifts a card while a swipe scrolls, a lifted card auto-scrolls on both axes (`RO().sortable` takes `alsoScroll`), every card target is 44 px tall, and a tap anywhere on a card opens the full-screen sheet. Gate: `test/board-layout-phone-browser.test.mjs` (Chromium at 375 × 812 and WebKit iPhone 15).
