- Undo reverts only your own writes. Each undo step sits on the stack of the actor that wrote it, an automation's step on the stack of the actor whose write fired it, and each actor keeps its newest 200 steps. Cmd+Z, `weave undo`, `weave_undo` and `POST /api/undo` used to revert whoever wrote last, another person's or an agent's write included, and the history preview listed everyone's steps (Issue #1692).
