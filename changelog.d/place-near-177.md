- **Popovers share one placement rule** (Feature #177): the chip popover, the search picker and the date picker each carried a copy of the drop-below, flip-above math with different edge clamps. They now place through `placeNear(pop, anchor, { gap })`, which keeps every popover 8 px inside the viewport on all four sides. A chip popover opened from a chip near the top of a short window no longer flips above the top edge, and a picker opened from a cell scrolled past the left edge stays on screen. Gate: `test/place-near.test.mjs`.
