- Share is one button and one pane everywhere. The Share up-arrow now sits in the header of the workspace, every space, table, record page, docked record and share page, beside the ⋮ and in its borderless style, and opens the docked share pane for that page. The old Share… dialog and the Share… rows on the table and record menus are gone. A record's pane lists the table and space links that already reach it, a second page's Share button takes the pane over instead of only closing it, and the button carries a 44 px touch target on phones (Issue #765, Issue #1222).
- A narrow docked record keeps its breadcrumb inside its box: once the middle of the trail has folded into "…" and the first crumb, the last-but-one and the current crumb's minimum width still do not fit, the last-but-one folds into "…" too, so the current crumb keeps its #id and part of its name (Issue #765).
