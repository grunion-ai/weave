- A long text or Name cell now expands in place. Rest the pointer on it for 400 ms and the value spills right on its own line from the cell's top-left edge, over the neighbouring columns, and stops at the grid's right edge with a fade where it runs out of room. Clicking it opens the editor in the same spot as a box that runs to the grid edge, then wraps downward and grows as you type; Enter saves and Esc puts the value back, as before. The hover copy used to float above or below the row and the click opened a second, clipped one-line editor under it. Chips, dates and descriptions keep their pop, and touch screens get no hover copy.
