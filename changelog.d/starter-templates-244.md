- **Three starter templates from the use cases people asked for** (Feature #244, Issue #605): onboarding step 3 and the first-run empty state now offer **Money**, **Work** and **People**, in that order, and Docs is gone (weave already has a document kind). Money is personal finance: Transactions (Date, Amount in dollars, Category, linked to an Account and a Month), Accounts, Recurring (linked to the transactions that paid it), Income (linked to a Month) and Months, where each month row rolls up **Spent** from its transactions and **Earned** from its income, and a **Net** formula subtracts one from the other. Work keeps the Tasks table (Status workflow, Due, Priority), and People keeps Companies and Contacts linked by Company. Each template is now a `weave_build` spec (Feature #253), so it builds in one call with a few sample rows dated to the month it is built in: the empty state sends one `POST /api/build`, and `POST /api/onboarding` runs the same build server side. `steps()` and its per-field REST walk are gone. A card's title is its space's name, so the card and the sidebar call it one thing. The empty state's lead line says row where it said record. Linking a transaction to its month is by hand until Feature #245. Gate: `test/starter-core.test.mjs` (each template builds; two transactions and one income linked to a month compute Spent, Earned and Net), `test/onboarding.test.mjs`, `test/onboarding-browser.test.mjs` and `test/first-run-browser.test.mjs`.
