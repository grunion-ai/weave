- **Invite a new person to a workspace** (Issue #569): an architect opens **Members** on the workspace's home page, enters an email, picks a role (Editor, paid seat, by default) and gets a one-time sign-in link to send; the same section lists the pending invites with role, inviter and date, and revokes one. Opening the link and signing in at the provider makes the account at the invited role, named after the email's local part (`-2`, `-3` when taken), pins the provider's subject to it, spends the invite and lands the person where they were going, so a new person no longer dead-ends at "No account for this identity". The invite is the same hashed `wvi_` code as an account link (Feature #252): the provider is still asked for no email, and the invitee's email lives on the pending invite only, never on the account, in the audit log or in an export. A revoked, spent, expired or other-workspace link answers `410`; a sign-in with no invite gets the refusal page as before. weave sends no invite email yet. Also `POST`/`GET /api/invites`, `DELETE /api/invites/:id`, `weave invite <email>`, `weave invite list`, `weave invite revoke <id>` and MCP `weave_accounts` `invite`, `invites`, `revoke-invite`.
