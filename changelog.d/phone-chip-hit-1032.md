- **Phone list chips get a 44 px hit area** (Issue #1032): on a phone the list row's state mark was a 65x20 target and its value chips 42x24, and a tap a few pixels right of the state opened the row. `weavePhoneRow.hitBoxes` now gives the state mark and every chip a box of at least 44x44 around the drawn pill, which keeps its size, slides it inside its row and splits every gap with a neighbour (another chip, the #id link, the name) at its midpoint, on whichever axis leaves both boxes biggest. At Standard density the name keeps its two-line slot, so the chip line sits 58 px down in every row and the state band and the chip band are 44 px each. A tap now routes by the point the finger touched down, because Chromium and WebKit move the synthesized click up to 9 px toward a nearby clickable row. Compact and wrapped rows are too short for two 44 px bands and split the gap at its midpoint. Gates: `test/phone-chip-hit-browser.test.mjs` and the `hitBoxes` cases in `test/phone-row.test.mjs`.
