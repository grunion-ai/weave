- `⌘Z` (Ctrl+Z) in the app undoes only a change the same tab made. Each tab keeps the undo entries its own writes recorded: cell edits, pastes and fills, trashes, links and any other record write sent from it. `⌘Z` steps back that tab's newest gesture only when its entries are the newest on the undo stack; when another tab, person or agent wrote after it, the tab says "Changed since, so nothing was undone" and sends nothing. A tab with no writes of its own says "Nothing to undo" and sends no request. Before, a `⌘Z` after a cell edit sent a one-step undo of whatever was newest in the workspace, so it could revert another person's or an agent's write in any space, with a toast naming a record the reader never touched. A paste's Undo button counts the paste's own rows the same way, where it used to undo a fixed number of steps whoever had written in between (Issue #1692).
