- **An invite can go out by email** (Feature #216, Issue #569): with `WEAVE_MAIL_KEY` (a Resend API key) and `WEAVE_MAIL_FROM` both set, `POST /api/invites` and the Members form email the approved invite to the address typed, one `fetch` to `api.resend.com/emails` with no SDK, and answer `mailed: true`; Members then says **Emailed** in place of the link. A failed send still makes the invite: the answer carries `mailed: false` and Resend's reason in `mailError`, the server log names it without the address, and Members shows the link to copy with a toast. With either variable unset nothing is sent and the copy-the-link flow is unchanged. The accepted notice is not sent, since weave keeps no account email (Feature #252). Gate: `test/mail-send.test.mjs` and `test/invites-mail-browser.test.mjs`.
