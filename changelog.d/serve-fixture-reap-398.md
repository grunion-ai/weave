- **A serve-boot test that times out no longer hangs the suite** (Issue #398): the Handbook and Showcase boot tests spawn `weave serve` and wait for it to come up. When the wait timed out, nothing killed the server, its pipes kept the test file's process alive, and `npm test` sat silent for as long as 28 minutes. Both tests now go through `test/lib/serve-fixture.mjs`, which stops the server on every outcome, escalates a SIGTERM it ignores to SIGKILL after a second, and waits for it to close before the test ends.
