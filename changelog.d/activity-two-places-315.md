- Activity shows in two places: the Activity button in the record's toolbar, which opens the panel inside the record, and the Activity system table at #/activity. The Activity card in the record's side column, the Activity system field (its table column and its show/hide row) and each document's History button are gone. Tables that stored the Activity switch lose it on load. The References and Referenced by cards load with the record and open the side column on their own, as do comments. `weave_query` no longer takes `Activity` in `fields`; `weave_get_entity` and `weave_activity` still carry the history (Feature #315).
