- **A short welcome names the first workspace** (Feature #248): a person new to an empty instance gets one held dialog with three steps. Step one welcomes them, step two names the workspace with a default already filled in and focused (the account's first name, else the workspace's current name), and step three offers the Tasks, CRM and Docs starters or an empty workspace. Enter takes the primary button; Skip on every step and Esc keep the defaults and land in a working workspace. A typed name the engine would refuse is folded into one it accepts ("Acme Team" becomes `acme-team`), and a name another workspace holds is refused before anything is built. The welcome runs once per person, finished or skipped: the mark is on the account row (`onboardedAt`), or on the hub root's meta when nobody is signed in, so it does not come back on another device. It asks only when the person may rename the workspace (no role, or architect), this workspace has no tables of its own, and no other workspace they can open has any, the weave docs workspace aside; an existing person with a populated workspace never sees it, and a populated home does not even ask. New: `GET /api/onboarding` (`{show, name}`) and `POST /api/onboarding` (`{name?, template?}`, which builds the starter server side through starter-core's `steps()`, renames, and marks), `Weave#onboardedAt` and `Weave#markOnboarded`, and `WeaveStarters.workspaceName`. Gate: `test/onboarding.test.mjs` (10) and `test/onboarding-browser.test.mjs` (both themes, every skip, 375 px).
