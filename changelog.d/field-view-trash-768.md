- Deleting a field or a share page now moves it to the trash. Restore brings a field back with its values, its column position, its place, width, filter and sort in every table view, and for a relation its inverse and every link. A share page comes back with its share links. The Trash page lists both, and `?hard=1`, `hard: true` or `--hard` still purges. New doors: `POST /api/tables/:table/fields/:field/restore`, `POST /api/views/:id/restore`, `GET /api/views?trash=1`, the `weave_restore_field` tool, `weave_views` action `restore`, and `weave field restore` / `weave view restore` (Issue #768).
