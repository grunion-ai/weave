- Inline fields take their padding and type size from two tokens, `--wv-inline-pad` and `--wv-inline-fs`, at one of two densities: grid (1 px 4 px) and record field row (2 px 6 px). The record summary strip joins the grid density, so its fields move from 12 to 13 px with 4 px side padding, and a date on the record page loses its extra form padding: 5 px 8 px becomes 2 px 6 px in the field rows and 1 px 4 px in the summary (Issue #786).
