- **AES-GCM decryption demands the full 16-byte tag** (Issue #533): the keystore and the backup archive both passed `setAuthTag` without telling `createDecipheriv` how long the tag must be, and Node then accepts a tag cut to 12, 8 or 4 bytes, each cut lowering the forgery cost. Both sites now pass `authTagLength: 16`; `test/security/gcm-tag-length.test.mjs` proves a keystore entry with a 12-byte tag is refused.
