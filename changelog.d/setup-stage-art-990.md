- **The setup page art keeps the mark at one size in any window** (Issue #990): the stage beside the sign-in pane drew an 816 x 528 landscape art with `object-fit: cover`, so a narrow or portrait window (900 x 1000, an iPad held upright) scaled it about 4x and showed two strands of the mark at poster size.
