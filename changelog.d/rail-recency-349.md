- The workspace rail lists workspaces in the order this browser last opened them, newest first, and the workspace you are in always sits first. Each workspace page load moves its name to the front of a `wv_recent` cookie (fifty names at most, same domain, path and lifetime as `wv_rail`), and `GET /api/workspaces` answers in that order; workspaces the cookie does not name follow in their usual order. The pinned weave mark keeps its place. A workspace whose session has expired shows as a grey dashed chip titled "<name>: session expired, click to sign in" and still opens through sign-in (Feature #349).
