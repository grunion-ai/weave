- **The slash menu opens over the record header and the section head, not under them** (Issue #550): a menu that flipped upward was painted over by two sticky bands, because Vditor's own rule puts `.vditor-hint` at z-index 4 while the document section head sits at 5 (Issue #411) and the record header at 6. On a Task page at 1280x800 `document.elementFromPoint` over the menu's own box answered `TEXTAREA.name-edit`, `DIV.view-header` and `DIV.doc-section-head`, so the rows in those bands could be neither read nor clicked, and the word DESCRIPTION cut the menu in half. The menu is now z-index 9, clear of both bands and of the selection bubble (7) with its tooltip (8), and `attachHintClamp` clamps an upward menu's top to the record header's bottom edge — the page's header or the dock's — holding the bottom edge Vditor anchored beside the caret, so the menu gives up rows to its own scroll instead of sliding behind the title. The window cap moved to the sheet as `min(400px, calc(100vh - 24px))`, and a closed menu drops the shrink so the next opening is measured at that cap. Gate: `test/slash-menu-stack-browser.test.mjs` (hit-tests every band of an open upward menu, in the gate's browser and in WebKit, both themes).
